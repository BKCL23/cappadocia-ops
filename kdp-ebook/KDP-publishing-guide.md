# KDP Publishing Guide — Cappadocia Travel Guide

Everything you need to get this book live on Amazon Kindle. Follow it top to bottom; it takes about an hour the first time.

---

## 0. Before you start (one-time setup)

1. Go to **kdp.amazon.com** and sign in with your Amazon account (create a free KDP account if you don't have one).
2. Complete your **Account Info**: this is the part people skip and then don't get paid.
   - **Tax interview** (required — Amazon walks you through it).
   - **Bank details** for royalty payments.
   - As a Turkey-based author, the tax interview determines withholding; a valid tax ID typically reduces US withholding under the treaty. Fill it in honestly and completely.
3. That's it — you can now publish.

---

## 1. Convert the manuscript to an upload file

KDP accepts **.docx** (easiest), **EPUB**, and a few others.

- Use the file **`Cappadocia-Travel-Guide.docx`** in this folder — it's already formatted for Kindle (title page, chapter headings, page breaks between chapters). Upload it as-is.
- KDP's online previewer will show you exactly how it looks on a Kindle. Check the Table of Contents links and the chapter breaks there before you hit publish.

> Tip: Kindle is **reflowable** — readers change the font size, so don't fuss over exact page layout. Clean headings and chapter breaks (already done) are what matter.

## 3. The cover (don't skip this — it sells the book)

The cover is the #1 thing that decides whether someone clicks. Options, easiest first:

1. **Amazon Cover Creator** — free, built into KDP, template-based. Fine for a first version.
2. **A design tool** (e.g. Canva has Kindle cover templates at 1600 × 2560 px).
3. **Have me generate one** — I can produce a Cappadocia balloon-sunrise cover image for you. Just ask and I'll create it.

Cover must-haves:
- **Portrait, ~1600 × 2560 px**, JPG or TIFF, RGB.
- A **strong balloon-over-fairy-chimneys sunrise** image (the instantly recognizable Cappadocia shot).
- **Title big and readable as a thumbnail** — most people first see it 2 cm tall on a phone. If you can't read the title shrunk down, it's too small.
- Use a photo you own the rights to, or a licensed/AI-generated one — **never** a random image off the internet.

## 4. Pricing and royalties

KDP has two royalty tiers:

| Royalty | You earn | List price must be |
|---|---|---|
| **70%** | 70% of list (minus a tiny delivery fee) | within KDP's qualifying range (roughly **$2.99–$9.99**) |
| **35%** | 35% of list | any allowed price |

**Recommendation:** list at **$4.99** (or **$5.99**) and choose the **70% royalty** option. That's the travel-guide sweet spot — high enough to earn a real royalty (~$3+/sale), low enough for an impulse buy. Enroll in **KDP Select** if you want (gives you Kindle Unlimited page-read income and promo tools; it requires Kindle exclusivity for 90 days — fine unless you plan to sell the ebook elsewhere).

Set the US price; KDP auto-converts for other marketplaces (you can fine-tune each).

## 5. Publish

1. Hit **Publish**. Review usually takes **up to 72 hours** (often much less).
2. The book goes live on Amazon worldwide.
3. **You can edit anytime** — fix a typo, update a price, swap the cover, add content — and republish. This is a living asset, not a one-shot.

## 6. After it's live — how it actually makes money

A published book with no traffic earns almost nothing. Here's the flywheel that makes it work — and where your hotel is the secret weapon:

1. **Point your existing audience at it.** Your hotel's guests, past guests, social followers, and website visitors are your first buyers and — more importantly — your first **reviews**. Reviews drive Amazon's algorithm.
2. **Put it in your funnel.** Link it from your hotel site, your booking confirmation emails, your WhatsApp follow-ups ("here's our free/paid guide to make the most of your trip").
3. **Content drives discovery.** The same Cappadocia balloon reels we talked about can mention the guide. One viral clip = a spike of sales.
4. **The book funnels back to the hotel.** The guide gently steers readers toward booking a cave hotel direct — ideally yours. So it earns twice: royalty *and* bookings.
