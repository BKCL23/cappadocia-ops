---

## ⚠️ Read this first (Amazon's rules — stay safe)

- **Never pay for reviews or buy fake ones.** It's against Amazon's policy and can get your book removed. It's also unnecessary — you have real past guests.
- **Don't offer a discount, freebie, or upgrade *in exchange for* a review.** You can *ask* for an honest review; you can't *reward* one. "If you enjoyed the guide, an honest review would mean a lot" = fine. "Leave a review and get 10% off your next stay" = not fine.
- **Asking past guests to buy and review is allowed** — just ask for honesty, not for a specific rating.
- Replace every `[AMAZON LINK]` below with your book's real Amazon URL once it's live. Consider a short link (e.g. a free bit.ly) so it's easy to paste into WhatsApp and stories.
